# ADR-008: MIT license

**Date**: 2026-06-08
**Status**: Accepted

**Context**: Need to choose a license.

**Decision**: MIT.

**Rationale**:

- Maximum adoption potential
- Compatible with Home Assistant (Apache 2.0), Ansible (GPL), and all other consumers
- Consistent with PyYAML, ruamel.yaml, yamlium, orjson
